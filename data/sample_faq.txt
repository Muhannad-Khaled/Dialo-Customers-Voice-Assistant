Dialo Customer Service — Frequently Asked Questions

== RETURNS & REFUNDS ==

Q: What is your return policy?
A: We accept returns within 30 days of purchase. Items must be unused and in original packaging. To initiate a return, visit our website and go to "My Orders", then click "Return Item". Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

Q: Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?
A: Yes! You can exchange any item within 30 days. Visit the exchanges page or contact our support team.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds typically process in 5-7 business days after we receive the returned item. Credit card refunds may take an additional 3-5 days depending on your bank.

== ORDERS & SHIPPING ==

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking email with a link. You can also log in to your account and visit "My Orders" to see real-time tracking.

Q: Do you offer international shipping?
A: Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination and are calculated at checkout.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. We do not accept cash or checks.

Q: Can I change or cancel my order?
A: Orders can be changed or cancelled within 1 hour of placement by contacting our support team. After that, the order may have already been processed for shipment.

== ACCOUNT & SUPPORT ==

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and follow the link sent to your inbox. The link expires in 24 hours.

Q: What are your customer service hours?
A: Our support team is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST. For urgent issues, use our live chat which is available 24/7 via this voice assistant.

Q: How do I contact support?
A: You can reach us via this AI voice assistant (available 24/7), email at support@dialo-demo.com, or phone at 1-800-DIALO on business days.

Q: How do I update my billing or shipping address?
A: Log in to your account, go to "Account Settings", and update your address under "Addresses". Changes apply to future orders only.

== PRODUCTS ==

Q: Do you sell gift cards?
A: Yes! Gift cards are available in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $200. They can be purchased online and delivered via email.

Q: Are your products covered by a warranty?
A: All products come with a 1-year manufacturer's warranty. Extended warranty options are available at checkout.

Q: Do you have a loyalty rewards program?
A: Yes! Our Dialo Rewards program gives you 1 point per dollar spent. Points can be redeemed for discounts on future purchases. Sign up is free.
